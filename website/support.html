Support

Get help with the QuickTUI app, your self-hosted QuickTUI server, your cloud account, subscriptions or Relay.

Email supportdualface@gmail.com X@dualface

What to include

  • Your QuickTUI app version and build number.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • What happened and the steps to reproduce it.
  • An error message or screenshot when useful, with sensitive information removed.

Protect your credentials. Never send passwords, access tokens, private keys, recovery codes or server URLs containing credentials.

Many common questions are answered in the FAQ and the install guide.

Subscriptions and account deletion

Apple processes App Store subscriptions; manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings. On iPhone and iPad you can delete your QuickTUI Cloud account in the app. If you signed in with Google, email us to delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel an active App Store subscription.
