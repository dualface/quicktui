QuickTUI compared

There are several ways to run AI agents or use a real terminal from your phone. Each of these tools overlaps with QuickTUI in different ways; here's a short take on each, with a detailed comparison page for every one.

QuickTUI is a native iPhone, iPad and Android (Preview) app that connects to the QuickTUI server on your computer and attaches straight to your running tmux or Herdr sessions, so you can run and supervise any CLI agent. It's end-to-end encrypted and direct by default, and the setup wizard installs the server over SSH and pairs for you. On iPhone and iPad it's free for one computer and Pro is a one-time purchase, and it's available on the App Store in China too; the Android app (Preview) is free during the preview. QuickTUI Relay is an optional subscription.

One by one

QuickTUI vs Moshi
A mobile terminal for AI agents that connects over SSH straight from your phone, with tmux and Herdr support (Pro-only), sold by subscription.
QuickTUI vs Happy
An open-source, chat-style remote control that launches agents like Claude Code through a wrapper and communicates through a relay.
QuickTUI vs Termius
A mature, cross-platform general-purpose SSH client for managing many servers, now with some AI agent conveniences.
QuickTUI vs Blink Shell
An open-source professional iPad terminal built around Mosh and SSH, for people who like to configure everything themselves.
QuickTUI vs dotMux
A lightweight tmux control-mode client that turns tmux windows and panes into native iOS UI, for a one-time price.
QuickTUI vs Omnara
Formerly an app for controlling agents from your phone, now a managed-agent platform.

The short version: choose QuickTUI to attach to the tmux or Herdr sessions on your computer and supervise AI agents from your phone, with easy setup, end-to-end encryption and a one-time price. If general-purpose SSH across many servers matters more, a terminal like Termius or Blink fits better.

Information about other tools is based on their websites and App Store listings as of September 2026. Check each tool's site for the latest details.
