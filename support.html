QuickTUI

Support

Get help with the QuickTUI app, your self-hosted QuickTUI server, cloud account, subscription, or relay service.

Email support dualface@gmail.com

What to include

  • Your QuickTUI app version and build number.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • A short description of what happened and the steps to reproduce it.
  • A sanitized error message or screenshot, when useful.

Protect your credentials. Never email passwords, access tokens, private keys, recovery codes, or server URLs containing credentials.

Subscriptions and account deletion

Apple processes App Store subscriptions. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. You can delete your QuickTUI Cloud account from within the app; deleting the account does not cancel an active App Store subscription.
